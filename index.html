About

The Story Behind RaffiaCell Sling

  • The Idea Begins

    The idea of RaffiaCell Sling started with the goal of creating a practical and stylish phone holder using natural raffia fibers. Inspired by Filipino craftsmanship, we aimed to design a product that is both eco-friendly and functional for everyday use.

  • Design and Creation

    Our team carefully planned, designed, and handcrafted the RaffiaCell Sling. Every detail, from selecting the raffia materials to weaving and assembling the product, was completed with creativity, teamwork, and precision.

  • Ready to Present

    After completing the product, we developed our branding, promotional materials, and website to introduce RaffiaCell Sling. This stage highlighted the product's quality, functionality, and commitment to sustainability.

  • Our Vision

    We envision RaffiaCell Sling as a trusted brand that promotes sustainable craftsmanship by creating stylish, functional, and eco-friendly phone accessories. Through our products, we aim to encourage the use of natural materials while inspiring people to appreciate and support locally handcrafted creations.

  • Our Mission

    Our mission is to create high-quality, handcrafted phone slings made from natural raffia that combine functionality, style, and sustainability. We are committed to promoting Filipino craftsmanship by offering eco-friendly products that provide convenience while encouraging the use of locally sourced and environmentally responsible materials.

Raw Raffia Material

The Natural Foundation of RaffiaCell Sling

The RaffiaCell Sling begins with high-quality natural raffia fibers, chosen for their durability, flexibility, and eco-friendly properties. Raffia is a renewable material that provides a lightweight yet sturdy foundation, making it ideal for handcrafted accessories. Using natural fibers also supports sustainable craftsmanship and promotes environmentally responsible products.
